## The goal

Each puzzle has a board of coloured glass panes and a small **target picture**. Slide the panes until the board matches the picture exactly. That's it. There's no timer, and every solve earns at least one star.

::shot level-1 | The first PaneSlide level, with a target picture above a small board of glass panes | The target picture sits above the board. Match it to win.

## The three rules

### 1. Every pane has a rail

A pane with **↔** moves only left and right. A pane with **↕** moves only up and down. Panes never turn and never leave their row or column.

### 2. Swipe, and the glass glides

Swipe a pane (or select it and press an arrow key) and it glides as far as it can. It stops when it reaches:

- the **edge** of the board;
- a pane on the **same kind of rail** (↔ panes can't overlap other ↔ panes, and ↕ can't overlap ↕);
- a **stone pillar** (from level 25);
- or it **sticks**, see rule 3.

::fig glide-edge

### 3. Glass sticks on new colour

Panes on crossing rails *can* overlap. A gliding pane **stops the moment it slides onto glass it wasn't already overlapping**. Sliding *off* glass never stops it. This is how you park a pane in the middle of the board: put a crossing pane in its way first.

::fig stick

More detail and examples: [how panes move](/guides/how-panes-move/).

## Colours mix like real filters

Where panes overlap, their colours combine:

::fig mix-chart

- cyan + magenta = **blue**
- cyan + yellow = **green**
- magenta + yellow = **red**
- all three = **black**

So a blue cell in the picture needs a cyan pane *and* a magenta pane there, one on each kind of rail. Read why in [colour mixing](/guides/colour-mixing/), and how to use it in [reading the target picture](/guides/reading-the-picture/).

## Par and stars

**Par** is the fewest swipes that solve the puzzle, proven by a computer search. Each puzzle has only one correct arrangement.

- ★★★ par or par + 1 (exactly par if par is 1 or 2)
- ★★ a few swipes more
- ★ any solve
- ✨ **Perfect**: exactly par

Undo takes back a swipe but still counts it. Blocked swipes, where nothing moves, are free. See [par, stars and perfect solves](/guides/par-and-stars/).

## Special glass and cells

Levels mode introduces nine special kinds, one at a time. Each one is taught on its own level with a short tip, then returns in later chapters. A level never mixes more than two (three only on rare challenge levels after level 100).

### 🔒 Locked glass (level 12)

Never moves. It still blocks same-rail panes, and crossing glass sticks on it. [Guide](/guides/locked-glass/)

::fig locked-stick

### 🎨 Two colours (level 18)

The picture uses just two of the three colours, so there's only one possible mix. [Guide](/guides/two-colour-puzzles/)

### 🧱 Stone pillars (level 25)

Block every pane like the board's edge. No glass can sit on them. [Guide](/guides/stone-pillars/)

::fig pillar

### 💎 Gems (level 33)

Slide any glass over a gem to collect it for a bonus shard. Gems sit along the shortest route. [Guide](/guides/gems/)

### ❄️ Ice (level 42)

Overlaps on ice don't count, so glass crossing other glass on ice keeps gliding instead of sticking. [Guide](/guides/ice-cells/)

::fig ice

### 🏖️ Sand (level 52)

Glass stops as soon as it slides onto more sand. Sliding off sand doesn't stop it. [Guide](/guides/sand-cells/)

::fig sand

### ➡️ One-way glass (level 64)

Glides only the way its single arrow points. Plan the stopper first. [Guide](/guides/one-way-glass/)

::fig oneway

### 🌫️ Frosted glass (level 78)

Hides its colour until you move it. Frosted panes always need to move, and the picture shows the true colours. [Guide](/guides/frosted-glass/)

### ❓ Mystery pictures (level 95)

Part of the target shows **?** at first. Every swipe reveals more, and the whole picture is visible after par − 1 swipes. [Guide](/guides/mystery-pictures/)

::fig mystery

## Levels, chapters and bosses

Levels are grouped into **chapters of 20**, each with its own scene, such as the Sunroom, Greenhouse and Lighthouse. The last level of each chapter is a **boss**: a bigger puzzle that restores a stained-glass window in your **Glass House**. As you progress you earn ranks, from Glass Spark at level 5 to PaneSlide Legend at level 500.

::shot level-40-boss | A PaneSlide boss level at the end of a chapter | Every 20th level is a boss.

## The daily puzzle

A new puzzle every day at midnight (your device's time), the same for everyone. Monday is gentlest; the weekend is hardest. Solve on consecutive days to build a streak, and share a spoiler-free result. Missed days can be played as practice in the [archive](/archive/). More in [the daily puzzle guide](/guides/daily-puzzle-habit/).

## Hints and getting unstuck

- **Undo** steps back one swipe at a time.
- **Hints** (Levels mode only) show the next best swipe from where you are. They never cost stars. You start with 3 and can hold 5.
- **"Stuck! Undo"** appears when a swipe has made the picture impossible.
- **Restart** (Levels mode) puts every pane back and resets the count.

See [undo, hints and getting unstuck](/guides/undo-hints-stuck/).

## Shards and rewards

Shards are earned only by playing, never bought:

| How | Shards |
|---|---|
| Each new star on a level | 2 |
| First perfect solve of a level | 3 |
| Each gem | 1 |
| Beating a boss | 15 |
| First daily solve each day | 5 |
| Each badge | 10 |

There's also a **daily gift** that grows across a 7-day week, and a free hint at level 5 and every 10 levels after. Spend shards in the **shop** on hints, new board styles (Oak, Mint, Rose, Midnight) and music moods (Rain, Music box).

## Settings

Open Settings (the gear) for:

- **Music**, **Sound** and **Buzz** (vibration) switches, and a music volume slider;
- **Patterns**: a texture for each glass colour, for colour-blind players or anyone who prefers it;
- **Progress code**: copy your progress to keep it safe, or load it on another device;
- **Install app**, where your browser supports it.

Your progress is stored only on your device. See the [privacy policy](/privacy/).

## Keyboard and screen readers

Press Tab to move between panes, and the arrow keys to slide the selected one. Each pane has a spoken label with its colour and the directions it slides.

How the archive works

  • Every day since puzzle #1 (27 October 2026) is here. Tap a day to play it.
  • Past days open in practice mode. You can solve them as often as you like, but they don't change your streak, your stats or your shards. Today's daily is the one that counts.
  • Days that haven't happened yet are locked, and the game will not open a future date.
  • Puzzles from before 30 October 2026 use the original drag rules; from 30 October every daily uses the glide rules. A daily never changes once it is out.
  • Dates follow your device's calendar, so a new daily appears at midnight where you are.

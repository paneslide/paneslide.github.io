New to PaneSlide, or just want a quick reminder? This glossary explains every term used in the game and the guides, in plain words, with links to the full guides.

## A to C

**Aha level.** A level built so that the most obvious move is not the best one. Levels ending in 4 and 9 (from level 7), and also 7 (from level 40), are aha levels. The game checks that a player who always makes the move that fixes the most cells right now cannot reach par. See [advanced strategy](/guides/advanced-strategy/).

**Archive.** A calendar of every past daily puzzle, for practice. Archive puzzles do not change your streak or give shards. See the [daily puzzle guide](/guides/daily-puzzle-habit/).

**Badge.** One of twelve achievements, each worth 10 shards. See [the House, ranks and badges](/guides/house-ranks-badges/).

**Black.** The colour of a cell where all three glass colours overlap.

**Boss.** Every 20th level. It mixes the chapter's main mechanics, and beating it restores the chapter's window in the House. See [boss levels](/guides/boss-levels/).

**Breather.** A gentler level that gives you a rest between harder ones. Levels ending in 1 and 6, from level 11, are breathers.

**Chapter.** A group of 20 levels set in a room, such as the Sunroom or Greenhouse, ending with a boss. See [Sunroom and Greenhouse](/guides/chapters-sunroom-greenhouse/).

**Challenge.** A tougher level marked with a trophy on the map. Levels ending in 9 are challenges.

**CMY.** Short for cyan, magenta and yellow, the three glass colours. See [primary colours](/guides/colour-wheels-primaries/).

**Cyan.** A blue-green glass colour. It takes away red light.

## D to G

**Daily puzzle.** One new puzzle for everyone each day, a little harder through the week. From 30 October 2026, dailies use the same glide rules as levels.

**Duo, or two-colour picture.** A level whose picture uses only two of the three glass colours. Introduced at level 18. See [two-colour puzzles](/guides/two-colour-puzzles/).

**Frosted glass.** A pane that hides its colour until it moves. Introduced at level 78. See [frosted glass](/guides/frosted-glass/).

**Gem.** A bonus item on some cells. Slide glass over it to collect a shard. Gems never affect stars. Introduced at level 33. See [gems](/guides/gems/).

**Gift.** A daily reward on a seven-day cycle, from 5 to 30 shards. See [shards, gifts and the Shop](/guides/shards-gifts-shop/).

**Glide.** How panes move. You choose a direction and the pane keeps going until something stops it. See [glide and stick](/guides/how-panes-move/).

## H to M

**Hint.** Shows the next best swipe on a shortest route. In Levels mode, hints never cost stars. You can hold up to five. See [undo, hints and getting unstuck](/guides/undo-hints-stuck/).

**House.** The place where your restored stained-glass windows are kept, one per chapter, each with 20 pieces.

**Ice.** A cell where glass never sticks, so it keeps gliding. Introduced at level 42. See [ice cells](/guides/ice-cells/).

**Locked glass.** A pane that never moves. It is useful as a stopper. Introduced at level 12. See [locked glass](/guides/locked-glass/).

**Magenta.** A pink-purple glass colour. It takes away green light.

**Mechanic.** Any special kind of glass or cell: locked glass, two-colour pictures, pillars, gems, ice, sand, one-way glass, frosted glass and mystery pictures.

**Milestone.** Level 5 and every tenth level. Milestones can give a free hint.

**Mix.** The colour where panes overlap: cyan + magenta make blue, cyan + yellow make green, magenta + yellow make red, all three make black. See [colour mixing](/guides/colour-mixing/).

**Mystery picture.** A level where part of the target picture is hidden at first and revealed as you swipe. Introduced at level 95. See [mystery pictures](/guides/mystery-pictures/).

## O to R

**One-way glass.** A pane that can only glide in the direction of its arrow. Introduced at level 64. See [one-way glass](/guides/one-way-glass/).

**Par.** The smallest possible number of swipes to solve a puzzle, proven by a full search. See [par and stars](/guides/par-and-stars/).

**Patterns.** A setting that adds a texture to each glass colour, for players who see colour differently. See [accessibility](/guides/accessibility/).

**Perfect.** Solving a puzzle in exactly par swipes.

**Pillar, or stone pillar.** A fixed block that glass cannot pass. Introduced at level 25. See [stone pillars](/guides/stone-pillars/).

**Progress code.** A code in Settings that copies all your progress, so you can move it to another device. See [install, offline and progress](/guides/install-offline-progress/).

**Rail.** The row or column a pane moves along. A ↔ pane moves left and right along its row; a ↕ pane moves up and down its column.

**Rank.** A title based on how many levels you have cleared, from Glass Spark (5) to PaneSlide Legend (500).

## S to Z

**Sand.** A cell that stops glass as soon as it slides onto it. Introduced at level 52. See [sand cells](/guides/sand-cells/).

**Shards.** The game's reward currency, earned by playing and spent in the Shop on hints, skins and music moods. They cannot be bought with money.

**Skin.** A cosmetic colour for the board's frame, bought in the Shop.

**Stars.** One to three per level, based on swipes. Three stars need par or par + 1 (exactly par when par is 1 or 2).

**Stick.** When a gliding pane slides onto a cell where it overlaps more crossing glass than before, it stops right there. This does not happen on ice.

**Streak.** The number of days in a row you have solved the daily puzzle on its own day.

**Swipe.** One move: a pane glides in one direction. Undo does not give a swipe back.

**Target picture.** The small picture showing how the finished window must look.

**Teach level.** The level that introduces a new mechanic, with a short explanation.

**Undo.** Takes back your last swipe, but the swipe still counts towards your total.

**Yellow.** A glass colour that takes away blue light.

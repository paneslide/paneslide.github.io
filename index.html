A calm colour puzzle, new every day

PaneSlide is a free sliding puzzle made of coloured glass. Swipe the panes along their rails until the board matches the small picture. Cyan, magenta and yellow glass mix where they overlap, just like real colour filters, so blue, green, red and black only appear where panes cross.

There is one new daily puzzle for everyone, an archive of past days, and endless levels that teach nine kinds of special glass one at a time. New here? Read how to play or browse the guides.

Contact us

Questions, bug reports, ideas and privacy requests are all welcome.

Email

The best way to reach us is by email: paneslide.game@gmail.com

We are a very small team, so please allow a few days for a reply. We read every message.

Helpful things to include

If you are reporting a problem with the game, these details help us fix it quickly:

  • what you were doing (for example "Level 42" or "the daily for 3 November");
  • what you expected to happen and what happened instead;
  • your device and browser (for example "Android phone, Chrome" or "iPad, Safari");
  • a screenshot, if you can.

Privacy requests

PaneSlide does not have accounts and does not store your game progress on any server. Your progress lives in your own browser, and you can delete it at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser settings. If you have a question about privacy, or want to make a request under a data-protection law, email us at the address above and see the Privacy Policy for details.
